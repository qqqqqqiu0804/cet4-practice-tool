## 二、语法易错点

### 1. 时态错误
- ❌ China is take great importance
- ✅ China is paying increasing attention（现在进行时：be + doing）

- ❌ the compulsory education law have take effect
- ✅ the Compulsory Education Law took effect（过去时）

- ❌ quality improves significantly
- ✅ quality has improved significantly（现在完成时，表示已经发生的变化）

- ❌ government always implement
- ✅ government has been promoting（现在完成进行时，表示持续动作）

### 2. 主谓一致
- ❌ the law have take effect
- ✅ the law has taken effect / took effect（第三人称单数）

- ❌ children could not pay
- ✅ children do not need to pay

### 3. 冠词缺失
- ❌ with series measure
- ✅ with a series of measures

- ❌ is the effective path
- ✅ is an effective way（泛指用 an）

### 4. 词性搭配
- ❌ let all children can have
- ✅ ensure that every child has（let 后面不加 can）

- ❌ to let all children can have the chance
- ✅ to ensure that every child has the opportunity

### 5. 拼写错误
- ❌ educaton → ✅ education
- ❌ pepole → ✅ people
- ❌ achieveing → ✅ achieving
- ❌ receiveing → ✅ receiving
- ❌ requestion → ✅ requirements / needs

## 三、句子结构问题

### 1. 逗号拼接句（两个独立句子不能用逗号连接）
- ❌ ...have take effect, after persistent efforts, achieveing the goal...
- ✅ Since the law took effect, the goal has been achieved through persistent efforts.

### 2. 简单句堆砌 → 升级为复合句
- ❌ children start primary school when they reach six years of age, from primary school to junior high school receiveing nine years compulsory education
- ✅ children start primary school at the age of six and receive nine years of compulsory education from primary school to junior high school

### 3. 冗长表达 → 简洁表达
- ❌ when they reach six years of age → ✅ at the age of six
- ❌ let all children can have the chance → ✅ ensure every child has the opportunity

## 四、专有名词规范

- China 句首必须大写
- Chinese 中国人的/中国的（不能用 China 作形容词）
- the Compulsory Education Law 《义务教育法》要大写
- China's 中国的（所有格）

## 五、四级翻译高频句型模板

| 中文意思 | 英文模板 |
|---------|---------|
| 一直致力于 | has been committed to / has been dedicated to |
| 以使/以便 | so as to / in order to / to ensure that |
| 自...以来 | Since + 时间点, 主句用现在完成时 |
| 经过不懈努力 | through persistent / unremitting efforts |
| 无需/不必 | do not need to / are not required to / are not obligated to |
| 随着...的实施 | with the implementation of... |
| 显著提高 | has improved significantly / has been significantly enhanced |
| 发挥更大的作用 | play a greater role in |
| 享有...的机会 | have the opportunity to / have access to |
| 符合中国国情 | suit China's national conditions / adapt to China's reality |
